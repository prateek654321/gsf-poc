Why traditional e-commerce architectures fail in automotive aftermarket, and how AEM EDS solves it.
01. The Fitment Barrier
Over 70% of automotive customer drop-offs occur if vehicle identification is slow or inaccurate. Customers must know with 100% certainty that a brake pad or filter fits before purchasing.
Critical Conversion Factor
02. Monolithic Performance Drag

FITMENT ENGINE
Real-Time Vehicle Registration (VRM) Flow
⚡ 100 PAGESPEED
From UK license plate input to guaranteed SKU fitment set in milliseconds.
STEP 1
Plate Validation
UK regex validates format in yellow search box. Sanitizes spaces, casing, and invalid characters.
STEP 2
DVLA Lookup
Calls /vrmdetails endpoint. Resolves Make, Model, Year, Engine, Fuel, and issues active sessionId.
STEP 3
SKU Resolution
Cross-references category component IDs with /skus to return exact compatible parts list.
STEP 4
State Broadcast
Persists to localStorage & cookies. Dispatches gsf:vehicle-changed event. Saves to "My Garage".
✔ Dynamic MOT & Road Tax Alerts
Calculates expiry dates and urgent service alerts directly from registration plate.
✔ Cascading Manual Vehicle Select
6-tier fallback: Make → Model → Year → Engine → Body → HP ratings via /vehiclemetadata.
✔ "My Garage" Multi-Vehicle Switcher
Sticky header dropdown enabling trade users and families to toggle saved cars in 1 click.

PRODUCT LISTING PAGE (PLP)
Dual-View Category Routing

• Sidebar Facets: Dynamic Brand filtering using Magento "quality" attribute codes (EBC, Bosch, Borg & Beck).
• Brand Sequence: Initial card ordering perfectly mirrors GSF preproduction catalog sequence.

• Dynamic Fitment Banner: Prominent green "Guaranteed to Fit [Vehicle]" or high-visibility red mismatch warning.

• Pricing & Promo: Transparent inc/ex VAT display and automatic coupon application (PARTS30).
